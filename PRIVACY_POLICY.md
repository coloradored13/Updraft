# Updraft - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 2026

## The short version

Updraft does not collect or share any personal data. Your gameplay data stays on your device. The web version may use privacy-respecting, cookie-free analytics (Plausible) that collect only aggregate visit counts — never anything personal.

---

## What data does Updraft store?

Updraft saves a small amount of gameplay data to your device's local storage so it can remember your progress between sessions:

- **High score** - Your highest altitude reached
- **Flight count** - How many times you've played
- **First-play flag** - Whether you've seen the tutorial hint
- **Recent struggle count** - A small counter that helps the game adjust difficulty if you're having a tough time (resets automatically)

That's the complete list. There is nothing else.

## Where is this data stored?

All data is stored in your browser's `localStorage` (for the web version) or on-device app storage (for mobile). It never leaves your device.

## Does Updraft connect to the internet?

The iOS and Android apps work entirely offline. There are no servers, no API calls, no network requests during gameplay.

The web version connects to the internet to load the game initially and may send anonymous page-view events to Plausible Analytics (see above). After the first load, the web version also works offline.

The only other network activity is your device checking for app updates through the normal app store process.

## Does Updraft use analytics or tracking?

The **web version** may use [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io), a privacy-respecting, cookie-free analytics tool. Plausible collects no personal data, uses no cookies, and is fully GDPR/CCPA compliant. It tells us only aggregate information like how many people visited and how many games were played — never who you are or anything about you individually.

The **iOS and Android apps** have no analytics whatsoever. No data leaves your device.

There are no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, no crash reporting SDKs, and no telemetry on any platform.

## Does Updraft show ads?

No. There are no ads, no ad SDKs, and no ad networks.

## Does Updraft have in-app purchases?

No.

## Does Updraft use cookies?

No. The web version uses `localStorage` only, which is not a cookie and is not shared with any third party.

## Does Updraft share data with third parties?

No. Since no data is collected, there is nothing to share.

## Children's privacy

Updraft does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The game contains no social features, no chat, no user-generated content, and no external links during gameplay.

## How to delete your data

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

- **Web:** Clear your browser's local storage for the Updraft site, or use your browser's "Clear site data" feature
- **iOS:** Delete the app. All stored data is removed with it
- **Android:** Delete the app, or go to Settings > Apps > Updraft > Storage > Clear Data

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app and posted at the same URL. Given that the game collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
