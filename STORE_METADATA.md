## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Some days you just need to fly.

Updraft is a calm, meditative game about a paper airplane drifting upward through an ever-changing sky. There are no timers. No ads. No lives to buy back. Just you, the wind, and the quiet challenge of climbing higher.

**How it works:**
Tap to change direction. Catch wind currents to gain altitude. Miss them and the breeze fades. That's it. Simple to learn, deeply satisfying to master.

**A sky that evolves with you:**
As you rise, the world transforms. Dawn's soft pink gives way to cerulean blue, then golden amber, twilight indigo, and finally the deep stillness of night. Each phase brings its own music, its own feeling, and its own gentle challenges.

**Wonder over score:**
Altitude is the one number that matters, but the real reward is what you see along the way. Break through cloud layers in flashes of white light. Watch distant birds drift past. Read quiet messages etched into the sky. Reach the summit and watch your airplane become a constellation among the stars.

**Designed for calm:**
- No red warning screens
- No punishing fail states
- No anxiety mechanics
- Gentle audio that responds to how you fly
- The game adapts to you, not the other way around

**Things you'll discover:**
- Five sky phases from dawn to night, each with unique music
- Wind current streaks that build momentum
- Thermal breathing zones where the air carries you
- Birds, kites, storm clouds, and crosswinds to navigate
- An adaptive camera that gives you more room when you need it
- A paper airplane constellation waiting at 10,000 meters
- A familiar friend at high altitude (if you try to coast)

**Your data stays yours.** High scores and flight count are stored locally on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The web version uses cookie-free, privacy-respecting analytics (Plausible) for aggregate visit counts only.

Built with care for people who need a break.
